The Phoenix mark and name lockup: two mirrored pairs of quarter-circle arcs that rise from the lower corners and cross in the middle, read as wings and as a flame.

Use it once per screen, in the header or on a splash. The files and the ink of each are in `assets/Logos/README.md`.

## What the consumer provides

- The ground it sits on (Light or Dark theme, or an `accent` fill), to pick the file.
- For the lockup, the name `Phoenix` set in the `title` style in `ink`.

## Rules

- **Mark alone:** at least 24px tall. Below that the arcs close up; use `phoenix-app-icon.svg` at 32px or more for a favicon.
- **Lockup:** the mark on the left, 28px tall next to a 36px `title` line, a `space-2` gap, vertically centered. Left-align the lockup with the page content.
- **Clear space:** a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- **Color:** `phoenix-mark-ink.svg` on Light, `phoenix-mark-reversed.svg` on Dark and on `accent`. The accent files appear at most once per view.
- **Never:** recolor, outline, rotate, stretch, add a gradient, shadow or glow, animate it, or place it in a rounded-square tile (the app icon is the only tiled form).
- **Text first:** the name beside the mark is always real text, not part of an image.
