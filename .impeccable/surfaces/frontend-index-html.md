# Landing (frontend/index.html, #landing)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: judges and mentors first, then signers and the people they talk to. Action: "Sinab ko'rish" opens #translate (works in demo mode without a server).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one conversation between two people, and red is the line of meaning passing between them. Refuses the split hero with feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: True white ground, one committed vermilion red (#D9241C) that owns whole regions (the storyboard field, the closing call), ink tinted from the red. Geologica heavy display with tight tracking, Golos Text for reading. Rounded 20px media frames, hairline rules, liquid-glass bottom dock as the only glass on the page.

STORY: The visitor sees real hands say "Welcome", reads it in their own language, understands Phoenix turns signs into text and voice and back, scrolls through five moments of one conversation, then presses "Sinab ko'rish".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (logo, demo tag, UZ/RU/EN). Wide real-footage frame of two people signing "Welcome" (placeholder slot until filmed), the localized caption set huge directly under it like a subtitle with a red rule drawing in, then one row: headline and lead on the left, primary red button on the right. Dock floats at the bottom.

FORM: pinned by the team's brief. seed: none (pinned) "oq rang va qizil rang ... haqiqiy odam qoli va 2 ta odam qoli haqiqiy xush kelibsiz sign qilib turishi kerak pastida xush kelibsiz bolib tursin ... pastda dock apple dock ... glass liquid scroll qilganda storyboard bolsin". Signature interaction: sticky storyboard stage that changes scene as the five steps scroll; dock magnifies under the pointer. Motion grammar: masks and rises with exponential ease-out, one orchestrated hero entrance.
