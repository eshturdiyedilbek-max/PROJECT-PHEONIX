To add a clip: put the MP4 here and list it in `OWN` at the top of `js/learn.js`. Use only clips you have the right to publish (WLASL videos are not redistributable).

Words without our own clip link to Spread The Sign (RSL and ASL) and SignASL (ASL), which open in a new tab.
